Common questions

How do I play?

Steer the ball onto the moving paddle by tilting your device or by swiping in the strip under the paddle. You can change the control in Settings. Open "How to play" on the start screen for all the details.

I bought diamonds but did not get them.

Open Settings in the game and tap "Restore purchases", then make sure you are online. If they still do not arrive, email us with the date of the purchase.

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?

Open Settings, copy your Backup code and keep it safe. On the new device, paste the code in Settings to bring your progress back.

What is inside a chest?

Chests give gold, diamonds and sometimes skins. The chance of each reward is shown in the game before you buy a chest.
