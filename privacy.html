Privacy Policy

Bounzo · Last updated 27 October 2026
SupportPrivacy Policy

This policy explains what information the Bounzo app ("the app", "we") uses, and why. The app is made by Abduleziz Halife, an independent developer in the United Arab Emirates.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. We do not run our own servers that receive your data.

2. Information stored only on your device

  • Your game progress: scores, coins, diamonds, skins, upgrades and settings.
  • The player name you type for the scoreboard. It stays on your device.

This information never leaves your device unless you choose to copy your Backup code yourself. Deleting the app deletes this information.

3. Motion sensor

If you choose tilt control, the app reads your device's motion sensor to move the ball. This is used only while you play and is never stored or sent anywhere.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows optional rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers, including the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking when the app asks you.
  • Approximate location based on your IP address.
  • Information about ads you see and tap, and app diagnostics such as crashes and performance.

If you do not allow tracking, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised using the IDFA. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Where required by law (for example in the EU and UK), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown.

Google's use of this data is described in its policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. In-app purchases

Diamond packs are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to those payments: apple.com/legal/privacy.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your choices

  • Turn off tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Delete the app to remove all game data stored on your device.
  • Contact us with any question or request about your data.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
